Privacy Policy & Terms

Learn how My Medical Centers protects your personal health information, manages automated communications, and responsibly uses clinical AI technologies.

1. SMS Messaging Privacy Policy

By providing your mobile number to My Medical Centers, you consent to receive SMS text messages from us regarding appointment confirmations, scheduling reminders, preventative health notices, clinic updates, and relevant clinical intake information.

Consent to Receive Messages

By opting in via our intake forms or Ocean booking portal, you acknowledge and agree that you are providing express consent to receive operational and medical appointment SMS communications from My Medical Centers.

No Third-Party Marketing

Your personal health information, including your mobile phone number, is strictly protected under Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA). We will never share, lease, or sell your contact information to third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

Message Frequency

Message volume is directly related to your active clinical appointments and care requirements (typically between 2 to 6 messages per month when appointments are scheduled).

Opt-Out Instructions

You may withdraw consent and opt out of receiving automated SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a final confirmation and no further automated text communications will be sent.

Support & Assistance

For assistance or inquiries regarding our automated communications, reply HELP or contact our administrative team directly at patient@mymedicalcenters.ca.

Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your cellular provider and mobile service plan.

2. Responsible and Transparent Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

My Medical Centers 2

At My Medical Centers 2, we use artificial intelligence (AI) responsibly to support our healthcare team, improve administrative efficiency, and enhance the patient experience. Our AI tools assist with selected clinical and administrative tasks, allowing our physicians and staff to focus more time and attention on patient care.

How We Use AI

Our clinic uses AI-assisted technology for the following purposes:

  • Medical documentation and AI scribing: AI may assist with summarizing physician-patient conversations and preparing clinical notes for the medical record.
  • Reception and patient communications: AI may assist with answering routine inquiries, directing requests, and providing general administrative information.
  • Appointment booking and scheduling: AI may help patients request, arrange, or manage appointments, depending on the service being used.
  • Administrative filing and workflow management: AI may assist with organizing, categorizing, processing, and routing administrative information and requests.

Human Oversight and Medical Responsibility

AI is a support tool and does not replace our physicians, healthcare professionals, or staff.

All AI-assisted clinical documentation is reviewed and approved by the responsible physician before it is finalized in the official medical record. Our physicians retain responsibility for their clinical assessments, decisions, and recommendations.

AI-assisted administrative tasks may also be reviewed or handled by our staff when appropriate.

Privacy, Confidentiality, and Security

We are committed to protecting the confidentiality of our patients' personal health information. AI tools are used subject to applicable privacy requirements, including Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (PHIPA), and our clinic's privacy and security practices.

Access to personal health information is restricted to authorized individuals and approved purposes. We assess the privacy and security implications of the technologies we use and apply appropriate safeguards.

Patient Awareness and Consent

We believe patients have the right to understand how AI is used in their care and in their interactions with our clinic.

When an AI tool is used to capture or process a clinical conversation, patients will be informed and consent will be obtained as required before recording begins. Patients may ask questions about the process, how their information is handled, and what alternatives may be available.

Our Commitment

Our goal is to use AI thoughtfully, transparently, and responsibly to improve clinic operations while preserving patient privacy, professional accountability, and the human relationship at the heart of healthcare.

At My Medical Centers 2, technology supports care. Our healthcare professionals remain at the centre of it.
